Single Mom or Single Dad With Several Kids: Fair Parenting When Time Is Short

When you parent alone, 'equal for everyone' is unrealistic. A realistic standard works better and is kinder to you.

Let go of the guilt first

Single parents have limited hours and energy. What kids need is to feel seen, which doesn't take unlimited time.

Short, one-on-one moments

  • Ten minutes at bedtime with one child, rotating nightly.
  • Walk to school or the store with just one child.
  • A monthly one-on-one outing for each child.

Don't make the oldest a mini-parent

Eldest children often become helpers. Keep their role small and age-appropriate, and protect their time to be a kid.

Asking for help is parenting too

Family, neighbors, school and local single-parent or family support centers can help. Services vary by area, so check with your local office.

Frequently asked questions

How can a single mom treat her kids fairly?
Rotate short one-on-one time, explain differences and drop the aim of perfection.

What matters most for a single dad with two kids?
Steady routines, individual attention and a support network.

Where can single parents find support?
Local family support centers, schools and community services.
