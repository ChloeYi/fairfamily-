Raising Sisters: Avoiding Comparison and Helping Each Daughter Shine

Sisters are close, which also makes comparison easy. One 'Why can't you be like your sister?' can stay with a child for years.

Where comparison sneaks in

  • Grades, looks and personality
  • Hand-me-down clothes and belongings
  • 'Your sister never did this'
  • Expecting sisters to be best friends

Say it differently

  • Describe what this child did well, without mentioning the other.
  • Let them pick something new now and then.
  • Respect each daughter's friends, interests and space.

When sisters fight

Listen to each side, agree on rules for shared and private spaces, and don't force friendship. Respecting boundaries helps the bond last.

Frequently asked questions

How do I stop comparing my daughters?
Focus on each girl's own effort and strengths and avoid using one as the yardstick for the other.

Why do sisters fight so much?
Shared belongings, rooms and friends, plus competing for attention, are common causes.

Is it unfair to hand down clothes?
It can feel unfair if one child never gets anything new. Offer fair chances to choose.
