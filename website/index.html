How it works

Write it down, compare, get a next step.

STEP 01

Write one sentence — or snap a photo

Type what happened the way you’d say it. AI splits it into entries by child and category. Or upload a photo of a gift or receipt and it fills in the details.

  • Several things in one sentence are split automatically
  • Photos are read for item, amount and category
  • Log past events with the right date and age
STEP 02

Compare siblings at the same age

See what each child received at 6, at 7, at 8 — side by side. The fairness radar shows which kind of attention leans toward whom.

  • Age-matched comparison by money, birthdays, time and more
  • 8-area fairness radar for every child
  • Fairness score and alerts on the dashboard
STEP 03

Get a small, specific next step

The AI coach reads your family’s records and suggests what to do this week — who, what, and for how long. You can also ask it anything.

  • Insights for each child, based on your own records
  • Concrete suggestions, like 20 minutes × 3 a week
  • Warm and non-judgmental — no scores for parents
